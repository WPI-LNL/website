Brand Guide

We take great pride in the services we offer to the WPI community and beyond, and our brand has become fairly well-recognized. In addition to offering low-cost event services, we do our best to add a professional touch to everything we do — our approach to branding is no different. We want to make it easy for you to sport the Lens and Lights logo and use our name in event materials, while at the same time respecting our brand and any applicable legal restrictions. This guide covers everything you'll need to know when using any of LNL's brand marks.

Our Name

Here at WPI we love acronyms! That's why you may often hear people refer to us as LNL. When using our name in published material, feel free to use our full name, our acronym, or both. As a general rule, when using our acronym, it's often best to include our full name as well, unless your audience is already familiar with our brand.

Full name: capitalize the L's only

  • WPI Lens and Lights
  • Lens and Lights
  • Lens and lights
  • lens and lights

Acronym: all caps, it's an initialism

  • LNL
  • LnL
  • lnl

When choosing a font, be sure to use one that is clean and easy to read. Avoid fonts that may be considered unprofessional (Comic Sans, Wingdings). It is also recommended that you avoid the use of the “Stencil” font around our wordmarks.

Exclusion Zone

The logo, icon, and wordmarks should each be given an appropriate amount of space to ensure that they do not compete with surrounding type or images. As a general rule of thumb, we recommend sizing the exclusion zone such that it is at least half the height of the wordmark. When using a combination of wordmarks, this should be based on the largest component.

Give our logos and wordmarks room to breathe.

Recommended width of the exclusion zone: at least half the height of the wordmark.

Logo Size

Our minimum size requirements ensure that the impact and legibility of our logos are not compromised. Both our logo and abbreviated wordmark should be at least 50 px wide or 15 mm in print. All of our other wordmarks should be at least 100 px wide or 26 mm in print.

Affiliated Brands

You may not use the LNL brand together with any other brand for which LNL is not officially affiliated. You may, however, use our brand marks in your event materials (advertisements, etc.) if LNL is providing services for the event.

Links to third-party websites should never be displayed alongside LNL brand marks. Furthermore, hyperlinks to any LNL website should be treated as an LNL brand mark and as such should not be displayed in any context that may reflect poorly on our brand.
